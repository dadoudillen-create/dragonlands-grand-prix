Dragon-lands Grand Prix - a mashup of The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim and F1 2021

Needs your own copies of both games. Nothing from either game is included in this download:
the F1 2021 car, livery, engine sound and handling data are converted on your PC, from your own
F1 2021 install, the first time you press Play.

Built with
- Ego Engine Modding (ERP, mesh and engine-sound formats), MIT licence, Petar Tasev and contributors:
  https://github.com/EgoEngineModding/Ego-Engine-Modding  (ported to Python for the importer)
- papyrus-compiler by russo-2025, MIT licence (build tool, not shipped): https://github.com/russo-2025/papyrus-compiler
- niftools nif.xml (NIF format reference): https://github.com/niftools/nifxml
- universal-modder toolkit: https://github.com/rehan-remade/universal-modder
